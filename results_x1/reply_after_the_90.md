## The fault, and the decision

**At A3 the Trace is stamped one second after the time the wake gives.** The clock line takes the time of the record's last line (09:14:40). The plan's answers are taken just after it (09:14:41). So an answer in hand is dated after "the time of this moment".

- 13 sessions raised it, 6 Opus and 7 Sonnet, all at A3.
- No ruling rests on it: all 60 sessions at A3 ruled by the key.
- It is a fault of the harness, and not something a reviewer found about the work. By your rule a wrong ruling that traces to the view is a reason to stop. There was no wrong ruling, so I did not stop, but I did not fix it or rerun on my own either.

The fix is one line: give the moment the time after its last answer. The choice:

1. **Leave the 60 sessions and state the fault as a limit.** The note does this now. I would choose this, since no ruling moved. The cost is that the "raised" counts at A3 carry 13 remarks that are about the harness.
2. **Fix it and run the 60 sessions that reached A3 again,** for about $23. The 30 sessions that ended at A1 never saw the fault and would stand.
